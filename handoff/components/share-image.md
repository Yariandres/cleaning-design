## Slots
| Slot | Copy key | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `og.title` | A short page name. **Never the H1 string, never the `<title>`, never a price** (DESIGN.md → Imagery; the stroller `<title>` contains "od 80 zł", which comes from data and would go stale inside a picture). No-photo: up to 3 lines at 80px (about 13 characters a line); a longer title uses 64px and up to 4 lines. Photo: 3 lines at 64px, 54px for 4 |
| Line | `og.line` | Two short facts, a dot between them. Optional |
| Photo | — | Photo variant only: 440 × 518, `object-fit: cover`, 24px radius, the owner's own photo, no people |
| Logo | — | `handoff/brand/logo/logo.svg`, unchanged. On the teal ground it sits on a white label (22px radius); there is no reversed logo |

`og:image:alt` = copy key `og.alt`.

## No-photo variant
The logo's own layout at full size: the chamomile on the left (white petals, sun centre, bleeding off the edge), the words on the right, on `primary-500`. Title in white Bricolage 800 (6.2:1), line in white Figtree 700 at 32px, dot `primary-200`. The sun appears once (the flower's centre).

It is built for how it is really seen:
- **About 400px wide** (WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook): the title is 27px tall type, the line 11px; both readable (`screens/29`, top row).
- **Centre-square crop** (WhatsApp's and Telegram's small thumbnail): the logo, the whole title and the line all sit inside the middle 630px (x 285–915), so the square shows a complete card, not half a headline (`screens/29`).
- 2:1 crop (X/Twitter): nothing is lost.
- File: PNG, 65 KB (WhatsApp ignores heavy images; keep any re-encode under 300 KB).

## Photo variant
Unchanged, so a photo drops in later without redesign. Two honest limits, measured on the same sheet (`screens/29`, bottom row): at 400px its 24px line is too small to read, and in a centre-square crop the title is cut and the logo is lost. Until the owner's photos exist and are worth showing small, the no-photo variant is the better share image. If the photo variant is used later, it should be revisited with a real photo in hand (a separate, small job).

## Other pages
Homepage v0 uses the **same no-photo variant** with its own `og.title` (to come with its SEO brief). Nothing else changes; `og-test-long-title.html` in the prototype folder shows the 4-line size with a dummy title.

## For the developer
- For the early launch the simplest correct thing is to **serve the rendered PNG as a static file** for the stroller page. Generating per page can follow.
- If the template is generated with `nuxt-og-image`: its default renderer (Satori) does not read WOFF2 or variable fonts and supports only a subset of CSS. The template uses flexbox only and one inline SVG, but it needs static TTF/OTF cuts of Bricolage Grotesque 800 and Figtree 700 (both SIL OFL), or the browser renderer. Please confirm which.
- Do not let the module default the title to the page `<title>`.
